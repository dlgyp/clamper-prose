# Clamper Prose: Before-and-After Examples

These examples are **fictional**. Their dates, events, and circumstances are invented solely to demonstrate style; they are not actual chapter announcements.

## 1. Registration reminder

**Plain**

> Registration closes Friday. Please sign up before the deadline to avoid the late fee.

**Clamper Prose**

> Gentlemen who have thus far entrusted their registration to the mercies of Providence are advised that **Friday is the deadline**. Providence, having shown little interest in bookkeeping, will not be paying your late fee. Attend to the matter while the contents of your purse remain intact.

**Why it works:** The central instruction remains obvious. The joke targets procrastination, not the reader's dignity.

## 2. Ordinary fraternal note — not a proclamation

**Plain**

> Thanks for helping set up the tables yesterday. We couldn't have done it without you.

**Clamper Prose**

> My thanks for yesterday's assistance with the tables. What appeared, on paper, to be a simple exercise in furniture arrangement developed the complexity of a territorial land dispute. Your intervention saved both the afternoon and what remained of my reputation for competent planning.

**Why it works:** Warm, modestly inflated, and personal. No mock decree or theatrical sign-off.

## 3. A ceremonial announcement

**Plain**

> The annual historical gathering is coming soon. Watch for details.

**Clamper Prose**

> Let it be known to all persons of dubious distinction that preparations for the annual historical gathering have commenced, notwithstanding several spirited objections from the calendar itself. Further particulars shall be delivered when the committee has agreed upon them, or at least ceased arguing long enough to put them in writing.

**Why it works:** The elevated register suits the genre. It does not manufacture a date, location, or price.

## 4. A historical feature

**Plain**

> The old wagon road connected settlements and helped transport supplies. Its route changed over time.

**Clamper Prose**

> The wagon road performed the indispensable service of connecting settlements whose inhabitants had, until then, been obliged to regard one another from a considerable distance. Its course changed over the years, as roads are inclined to do when geography refuses to consult the surveyor.

**Why it works:** Satirical observation enriches the sentence without adding fabricated historical details. Any real road's history still needs sources.

## 5. What to avoid

**Overdone**

> HARK! HEAR YE! By thunderous decree of the most exalted brethren, thou shalt forthwith and without delay register, lest a thousand curses befall thee!

**Better**

> Registration closes Friday. Even the most accomplished practitioner of procrastination is advised not to test the committee's patience—or the late-fee schedule.

**Why it works:** Mock-archaic is not medieval; jokes work better when the reader can find the point.

## 6. Responsible libations

**Plain**

> Refreshments may be available. Drink responsibly.

**Clamper Prose**

> Refreshments may be available to those so inclined. As always, exercise the good judgment that experience has occasionally demonstrated to be within our collective reach: **drink responsibly**, and make suitable arrangements for a safe journey home.

**Why it works:** Fraternal humor and responsible conduct coexist without suggesting that alcohol is required.
