# Using Clamper Prose with an AI Writing Assistant

This is an **unofficial public prompt companion** to [STYLE_GUIDE.md](STYLE_GUIDE.md). It describes a literary voice, not the policies or traditions of any particular ECV chapter.

## Reusable prompt

> Write in **Clamper Prose**, following the accompanying style guide. Combine E. B. Farnum-like florid frontier pomp with Mark Twain-like dry satire, understatement, and affectionate irreverence. Make the humor serve the information. Fit the genre: a personal note is not automatically a proclamation, and a routine reminder does not require a decree. Preserve clear, skimmable logistics. Verify historical claims and never invent event details, chapter traditions, or confidential ceremonies. If alcohol is mentioned, communicate responsible consumption without glorifying excess. Do not use Clamper Prose outside requests that explicitly call for it.

## Provide these inputs

For best results, specify the audience, purpose, genre, required facts, tone intensity, and desired length. Supply any chapter-specific terminology or restrictions privately; **do not paste confidential information into a public issue or pull request**.

Example request:

> Draft a 200-word event reminder in Clamper Prose for initiated members. The gathering is Saturday, November 14, at 6:00 p.m. at a venue to be confirmed. Registration closes November 10. Keep the deadline unmistakable, use mild pomp and one dry joke, and do not invent the venue or admission price.

## Revision prompts

- **Too pompous?** “Reduce ceremonial language by half, keep the Twain-like wit, and preserve all facts.”
- **Too plain?** “Add one comic circumlocution and one understated frontier comparison without increasing the length substantially.”
- **Hard to skim?** “Put date, place, price, and action in a compact logistics section. Do not invent missing information.”
- **Too generic?** “Remove formulaic ‘Hear ye’ openings, repetitive archaic vocabulary, and boilerplate proclamations.”
- **Too chapter-specific?** “Remove unverified titles and traditions; retain only broadly applicable ECV context.”

## Human review

Before publication, check every name, date, historical statement, attendance restriction, registration link, and price against authoritative sources. The author—not the model—is responsible for the final message.
